# OboDesk Market Evidence Log V1

Status: Manual record template only. Do not automate, import, sync, or use as proof of PMF by itself.

Purpose: Track first-round market evidence for the five MVP products across three core scenarios:

- AI Workspace
- Remote Work
- Long Hours Desk Setup

## Rules

- Fill this log manually after reviewing real evidence.
- Do not count views alone as validation.
- Keep screenshots, Shopify analytics exports, TikTok analytics, comment links, or observation notes as evidence references when available.
- Leave unknown values blank. Do not estimate.
- Record questions and objections in the user's words when possible.
- Do not write to Shopify, Product Center, metafields, or automation from this log.

## Core Scenarios

| Scenario | Target use case | Evidence to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Workspace | Desk setup for AI-heavy work, laptop + monitor, tools, notes, calls, and device organization. | Product-specific questions, setup clarity, accessory fit, PDP visits, add-to-cart. |
| Remote Work | Home office or hybrid work desk used for daily calls, focus work, and document/computer tasks. | Trust questions, price objections, shipping questions, repeat PDP visits, checkout starts. |
| Long Hours Desk Setup | Desk used for long sessions, comfort, organization, lighting, and reduced clutter. | Avoid health claims; watch for comfort-related questions, product fit, comparison with marketplaces. |

## Evidence Strength Guide

| Strength | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| None | No real user behavior or feedback yet. |
| Weak | Views, isolated comments, or internal assumptions only. |
| Medium | Repeated relevant questions, PDP visits, add-to-cart, checkout starts, or consistent user observations. |
| Strong | Orders, repeated buyer-intent signals, repeatable content-to-PDP-to-cart behavior, or repeated product-specific demand. |
