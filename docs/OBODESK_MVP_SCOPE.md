# OboDesk MVP Scope

Updated: 2026-05-20

Purpose: this document defines what OboDesk should and should not do during the current evidence-based MVP stage. It keeps the team focused on market evidence instead of expanding system complexity.

## Current Stage Judgment / 当前阶段判断

OboDesk is in the evidence-based MVP stage.

The project has meaningful infrastructure:

- A live Shopify storefront.
- A verified basic purchase path.
- Core collections and MVP product entries.
- Product Center v1.0 as the product asset hub.
- Product fact verification workflows.
- Safe PDP draft content.
- Codex-assisted local review and documentation.
- Clear boundaries against premature Product Center writes, Shopify metafields, and full automation.

The project does not yet have enough evidence to be treated as a launch-stage or scale-stage business:

- No stable record of target-user interviews.
- No documented content-to-PDP-to-purchase conversion loop.
- No product-level evidence of repeated add-to-cart, purchase, retention, repeat purchase, or referral.
- No customer support feedback loop tied to product decisions.
- No repeatable acquisition channel evidence.

## Current MVP Core Goal / 当前 MVP 核心目标

The current MVP goal is to prove whether a focused group of real shoppers understands, trusts, and buys OboDesk's core desk setup products without founder explanation.

The MVP should answer five practical questions:

1. Who is the clearest target user?
2. Which desk setup problem is painful enough to influence purchase behavior?
3. Which product or product set creates the strongest initial purchase signal?
4. Which PDP facts, images, claims, and objections affect conversion?
5. Which acquisition path can repeatedly bring relevant shoppers?

## Current Allowed Work / 当前允许做的事情

The following work is allowed during the evidence-based MVP stage:

- Keep the Shopify purchase path working.
- Fix storefront blockers that prevent browsing, add-to-cart, cart, checkout, or trust-page access.
- Maintain the MVP5 and core product facts.
- Continue manual product fact verification.
- Review Product Center sync drafts before any formal import.
- Improve PDP content only after facts are verified and human-approved.
- Record user interviews, support questions, objections, checkout blockers, and product questions.
- Run small manual content tests for TikTok, organic posts, or product-page copy.
- Produce weekly evidence summaries from Shopify analytics, customer messages, and content performance.
- Use Codex to inspect, summarize, validate, draft, and prepare review files.

## Current Explicit Non-Goals / 当前明确不做的事情

The following should not be done yet:

- Do not expand third-batch product volume before the current workflow and evidence loop are stable.
- Do not create Shopify metafields from draft content before field-level review.
- Do not write Product Center formal tables automatically.
- Do not let agents publish PDP copy directly.
- Do not automate Shopify-to-Feishu sync until validation rules, duplicate handling, and review paths are stable.
- Do not treat homepage polish, product count, or workflow speed as PMF.
- Do not build complex CRM, customer success, or growth automation before real customer volume exists.
- Do not optimize for scale-stage compliance or process depth unless a current risk requires it.
- Do not add new product categories without a written problem hypothesis and evidence plan.

## Evidence Required To Enter Launch Stage / 哪些证据才允许进入发布阶段

OboDesk may consider moving toward launch stage only after several of the following signals are present:

- A clearly defined target user segment repeatedly engages with OboDesk products.
- Shopify analytics show meaningful product-level behavior: PDP views, add-to-cart, checkout started, purchases, and repeat signals.
- At least one acquisition path produces relevant visitors repeatedly, not just one-off traffic.
- Users can understand OboDesk's value proposition without founder explanation.
- Customers or target users provide repeated feedback showing that OboDesk solves a real buying or setup problem.
- Product-level objections are known and addressed in PDP content, images, specs, or support material.
- The MVP product set has enough verified facts for safe PDP content and reduced claim risk.
- The support and feedback loop can be reviewed weekly without relying on memory.

Minimum evidence threshold before treating OboDesk as launch-ready:

- At least 3 consecutive weeks of product-level Shopify evidence for the core MVP products, including PDP views, add-to-cart, checkout started, and purchase data. Zero-purchase weeks can still be useful evidence, but they do not qualify as launch-stage proof.
- At least 10 target-user interviews or task-based shopping observations, with notes on current alternatives, purchase objections, trust blockers, and product comprehension.
- At least 20 valid user feedback items from interviews, support messages, PDP questions, TikTok comments, email replies, or post-purchase messages. Valid feedback must come from a plausible target user and mention a concrete product, scenario, objection, or desired outcome.
- At least one product or product set shows repeated buying intent, such as multiple add-to-cart events from non-founder traffic, checkout starts, purchases, or direct user requests to buy after viewing content.
- At least one content or acquisition path shows buyer intent, not only views. Valid signals include product link clicks, PDP sessions from content, add-to-cart events, checkout starts, purchases, or repeated product-specific questions.
- At least one weekly evidence review has connected user behavior to a concrete product/PDP/content decision, and that decision has been reviewed after the validation window.

## Signals That Require Pivot Or Return To Idea Stage / 哪些信号说明需要 pivot 或回到想法阶段

OboDesk should pause, narrow, pivot, or return to idea-stage validation if these signals appear:

- Users understand the products but do not see a reason to buy from OboDesk instead of marketplaces.
- Traffic reaches PDPs but add-to-cart remains consistently weak across products.
- Add-to-cart happens but checkout or purchase does not, and the reason is not a fixable trust, shipping, pricing, or payment issue.
- Users mainly ask for products outside the current OboDesk desk-system positioning.
- The clearest demand is for one product category, not the broader desk system.
- Safe factual PDPs reduce confusion but do not improve purchase confidence or behavior.
- Content channels generate views but not relevant shopper intent.
- Product fact gaps repeatedly block claims needed to sell the product safely.
- Operations consume founder attention without producing new market evidence.

Pivot review cadence:

- If 2 consecutive weeks produce no useful market signal from the active experiment, review the experiment design, traffic source, PDP clarity, and target user assumption.
- If 4 consecutive weeks produce no valid buyer-intent signal, pause expansion and run a formal pivot review.
- If repeated interviews show that the target user does not recognize the problem or prefers existing alternatives, return that direction to idea-stage validation.
- If the strongest evidence concentrates around one product category, consider narrowing the MVP instead of expanding the broader desk-system story.

## MVP Operating Rhythm

Recommended weekly rhythm:

1. Review shopper evidence: analytics, support messages, user interviews, content comments, product objections.
2. Update the problem evidence map.
3. Decide one narrow product/content/storefront experiment.
4. Define the expected result and validation date.
5. Run the smallest version manually.
6. Record what happened and whether the assumption changed.

## Current Priority

The current priority is not more automation. The current priority is to connect OboDesk's Shopify storefront, Product Center, and content work to real market evidence.
